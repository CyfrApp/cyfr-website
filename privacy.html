Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

AVOA respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes the information we collect and process when you use our website, mobile application, and related services (collectively, the “Services”), including account-based dance tracking, private journaling, cloud synchronization, and community features.

1. Information We Collect

Account and Profile Information

When you register for or use AVOA, we may process account details such as your email address, authentication identifiers, age eligibility information, and profile information you choose to provide, such as a username, display name, profile image, biography, and dance interests. Authentication information is used to help secure access to your account.

Dance Activity and Content

Depending on how you use the app, we may process dance class and practice logs, dates, styles, instructors or studios, duration, notes, goals, progress statistics, achievements, challenge activity, dance sessions, and photos or videos you choose to add.

Some content may be stored on your device, while other content may be stored using cloud services to support your account, synchronization, backup, and access across supported devices. Cloud synchronization and backup may be subject to technical limitations, including connectivity, storage capacity, and supported file formats.

Private dance journal entries and associated private media are stored separately from community-shared content and are subject to account-based access controls. Private journal content is not made publicly visible simply because it is stored using cloud services.

Community Activity

If you use community features, we may process profile information, posts, shared activities, comments, reactions, follows, follow requests, tagged users, dance session participation, invitations, and user blocking information.

Information you choose to share through community features may be visible to other users according to the app's available visibility and sharing controls. Please avoid posting sensitive information you do not want others to see.

Reports and Moderation Information

If you report content or another user, or if your content or account is reported, we may process information associated with the report, including account identifiers, reported content, report categories, descriptions, timestamps, and relevant content snapshots or media references.

We may also process moderator notes, report decisions, content removal records, account restrictions, and other moderation-related information. This information is used to investigate reports, enforce our Terms of Use, protect users, and maintain the safety and integrity of the Services.

Website, Communications, and Technical Information

If you contact us or subscribe to updates, we may collect the information you submit, such as your name, email address, and message. The Services and their providers may also process technical information necessary for operation, security, troubleshooting, and delivery, such as device or browser information, IP address, and diagnostic events.

2. How We Use Information

We use information as appropriate to:

  • create, authenticate, and maintain accounts;
  • administer account eligibility requirements;
  • provide dance logs, private journals, progress tracking, goals, challenges, achievements, and other app features;
  • enable profile and community features you choose to use;
  • store, back up, and synchronize information needed for the Services;
  • facilitate community interactions, dance sessions, and user-controlled sharing;
  • review reports, investigate violations, moderate content, and enforce account restrictions;
  • respond to questions, support requests, and account-related communications;
  • send product or marketing updates where you have subscribed or where otherwise permitted, with an option to unsubscribe;
  • maintain security, prevent misuse, diagnose issues, and improve the Services; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing and Visibility

Private dance journal entries and associated private media are not made publicly available merely because they are stored in your account. Content you choose to publish or share through social features may be available to other users according to the app's available visibility and sharing controls.

Profile information and community activity may be visible to other users depending on your account settings, the applicable feature, and your relationship with those users. You may manage available privacy settings and use in-app controls to block other users.

Other users may save, copy, or otherwise retain information you have shared with them. Changing visibility settings or removing shared content may not remove copies previously obtained by other users.

We may share information with service providers that help operate the Services, when necessary to comply with law or protect rights and safety, or with your direction or consent. Authorized personnel may access information when reasonably necessary to provide support, investigate security issues, review reports, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Photos, Videos, and Device Permissions

AVOA may let you select photos or videos for private dance journals, your profile, or community content. The app may request access to relevant device features or media only as needed for the actions you choose. You can manage permissions through your device settings.

Content uploaded to the Services may be processed and stored to provide the requested feature, including generating thumbnails, displaying media, and supporting cloud synchronization and backup.

Private journal media and community-shared media are stored separately and are subject to their respective access controls. Media you choose to share through community features may be accessible to other users according to the applicable visibility settings.

5. Third-Party Services

AVOA uses third-party service providers to support the operation and delivery of the Services. These may include:

  • Supabase: account authentication, database services, cloud storage, and associated backend functionality;
  • Resend: delivery of transactional emails, including account verification and password reset communications;
  • MailerLite: management and delivery of subscribed email communications; and
  • GitHub Pages: hosting and delivery of the AVOA website.

Service providers may process information necessary to provide their services under their own applicable terms and privacy practices, as well as our arrangements with them.

Information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country in which you reside, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

6. Email Communications

If you subscribe to AVOA announcements or marketing updates, we may use your submitted contact information to send those messages. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails through the unsubscribe link included in those emails.

Essential account or service communications, including account verification, password reset, security, and support communications, may still be sent where necessary.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the Services, maintain accounts, investigate reports, address security or legal obligations, and resolve disputes, subject to applicable law. Retention periods can vary by the type of information and the reason it is processed.

Reports and Moderation Records

To support community safety and consistent enforcement, AVOA generally applies the following retention periods to content reports and moderation records:

  • pending reports and reports under review are retained while the matter remains unresolved;
  • dismissed reports are generally retained for 90 days following dismissal;
  • resolved reports are generally retained for 365 days following resolution; and
  • moderation action history is generally retained for 365 days following the recorded action.

Records may be retained longer when subject to a legal hold or when otherwise necessary and permitted by applicable law. Retained report information may include content snapshots and media references, even when the original reported content has been removed.

Account and Content Deletion

AVOA provides an in-app account deletion option. When an account is successfully deleted, associated account information, private cloud journal data, community content, and uploaded media are deleted, subject to applicable retention requirements and technical limitations.

Certain information, including report records, moderation history, and information necessary to address legal or security obligations, may be retained following account deletion where legally permitted or required. Account identifiers may be removed from retained records where appropriate.

Deleting the app from your device does not necessarily delete information associated with your cloud account. Information retained in backups may remain until it is removed through applicable backup retention processes.

Privacy Requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You may submit privacy-related requests through our Support page. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests. Where available, you may also edit or remove information using controls in the app.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information handled through the Services, including account authentication, access controls, and restrictions on access to stored information and media.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for taking reasonable steps to protect your account credentials and devices.

9. Children's Privacy

AVOA is intended for individuals who are at least 13 years old. Individuals under 13 are not permitted to create an account or use the account-based Services.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through account registration. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information through the Services, we will take appropriate steps to restrict access and delete the information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Users between the age of 13 and the age of majority in their jurisdiction must comply with applicable age-related requirements, including obtaining permission from a parent or legal guardian where required by law.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to AVOA, please contact us through our Support page so we can review and address the matter.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as AVOA and its practices evolve. We will post the revised policy here with an updated effective date and provide additional notice when required by law.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, please visit our Support page or contact us at avoa.dance@gmail.com.
