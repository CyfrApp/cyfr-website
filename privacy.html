Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

AVOA respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes the information we collect and process when you use our website, mobile application, and related services (collectively, the “Services”), including account-based dance tracking and community features.

1. Information We Collect

Account and Profile Information

When you register for or use AVOA, we may process account details such as your email address, authentication identifiers, and profile information you choose to provide, such as a display name, profile image, biography, and dance interests. Authentication information is used to help secure access to your account.

Dance Activity and Content

Depending on how you use the app, we may process dance class and practice logs, dates, styles, instructors or studios, duration, notes, goals, progress statistics, achievements, challenge activity, and photos or videos you choose to add. Some content may be stored on your device, and some may be stored using cloud services to support your account and app functionality.

Community Activity

If you use community features, we may process profile information, posts or other shared activity, and interactions such as follows or engagement with other users. Information you choose to share through community features may be visible to other users according to the app's available visibility and sharing controls. Please avoid posting sensitive information you do not want others to see.

Website, Communications, and Technical Information

If you contact us or subscribe to updates, we may collect the information you submit, such as your name, email address, and message. The Services and their providers may also process technical information necessary for operation, security, troubleshooting, and delivery, such as device or browser information, IP address, and diagnostic events.

2. How We Use Information

We use information as appropriate to:

  • create, authenticate, and maintain accounts;
  • provide dance logs, progress tracking, goals, challenges, achievements, and other app features;
  • enable profile and community features you choose to use;
  • store and synchronize information needed for the Services;
  • respond to questions, support requests, and account-related communications;
  • send product or marketing updates where you have subscribed or where otherwise permitted, with an option to unsubscribe;
  • maintain security, prevent misuse, diagnose issues, and improve the Services; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing and Visibility

Your account and dance journal information is not necessarily public merely because it is stored in your account. However, content you choose to publish or share through social features may be available to other users. The visibility of individual profile fields and activities depends on the features and controls implemented in the app.

We may share information with service providers that help operate the Services, when necessary to comply with law or protect rights and safety, or with your direction or consent. We do not describe private journal content as publicly shared unless you choose a feature that makes it available to others.

4. Photos, Videos, and Device Permissions

AVOA may let you select photos or videos for logs, your profile, or community content. The app may request access to relevant device features or media only as needed for the actions you choose. You can manage permissions through your device settings. Content uploaded to the Services may be processed and stored to provide the requested feature.

5. Third-Party Services

AVOA uses Supabase services for app backend functionality, including account authentication and associated data services. These services may process information needed to provide the app. We may also use MailerLite to manage subscribed email communications, and GitHub Pages to publish our website. Service providers process information under their own applicable terms and privacy practices, as well as our arrangements with them.

6. Email Communications

If you subscribe to AVOA announcements or marketing updates, we may use your submitted contact information to send those messages. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails through the unsubscribe link included in those emails. Essential account or service communications may still be sent where necessary.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the Services, maintain accounts, address security or legal obligations, and resolve disputes, subject to applicable law. Retention periods can vary by the type of information and the reason it is processed.

You may request access to or deletion of your personal information through our Support page. Where available, you may also edit or remove content using controls in the app. Deleting the app from your device does not necessarily delete information associated with your cloud account. Certain records may be retained where legally required or otherwise permitted.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information handled through the Services. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not intend to knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that requires parental authorization or removal, please contact us through our Support page so we can review the request.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as AVOA and its practices evolve. We will post the revised policy here with an updated effective date and provide additional notice when required by law.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, please visit our Support page.
