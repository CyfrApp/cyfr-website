Community Guidelines

Last updated: November 25, 2026

AVOA is the home for dancers — a place to document your journey, celebrate progress, express yourself, and connect with others who share your passion for dance.

We welcome dancers of all backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and styles. These Community Guidelines help us maintain a safe, supportive, and creative environment while respecting individuality and artistic freedom.

These Guidelines apply to community content and interactions across AVOA, including profiles, posts, comments, photos, videos, and dance sessions. They supplement our Terms of Use.

1. Respect the Community

Everyone deserves to participate in the dance community without fear of harassment, intimidation, or abuse. We encourage constructive feedback, meaningful conversations, and respectful differences of opinion.

We do not allow:

  • targeted harassment, bullying, or intimidation;
  • credible threats, stalking, or encouragement of violence;
  • coordinated harassment or repeated unwanted contact;
  • hate speech or content promoting hatred, discrimination, or violence based on protected characteristics; or
  • attempts to circumvent another user's blocking or privacy controls.

Disagreement, constructive criticism, and honest feedback are not inherently violations of these Guidelines.

2. Celebrate Creative Expression

Dance is a form of artistic and personal expression. AVOA recognizes that choreography, music, costumes, performance, and creative discussions may involve mature themes, expressive language, or provocative artistic choices.

Ordinary profanity, explicit song lyrics, expressive choreography, and mature artistic themes are not automatically prohibited. We consider the context and nature of content when evaluating potential violations.

However, we do not allow:

  • pornography or sexually explicit content;
  • sexual exploitation or non-consensual intimate imagery;
  • graphic violence or content encouraging serious harm;
  • content that promotes or facilitates abuse or exploitation; or
  • otherwise unlawful content.

Artistic expression does not excuse harassment, exploitation, or other violations of these Guidelines.

3. Protect Minors

You must be at least 13 years old to create an AVOA account or use our account-based Services.

The safety of younger community members is especially important. We strictly prohibit:

  • sexual exploitation or sexualization of minors;
  • child sexual abuse material in any form;
  • grooming or inappropriate sexual solicitation of minors;
  • attempts to exploit, manipulate, or endanger minors; and
  • sharing information about minors in ways that threaten their privacy or safety.

Serious violations involving child safety may result in immediate account restrictions or termination and may be reported to appropriate authorities where required or permitted by law.

4. Respect Privacy and Consent

Respect the privacy, personal boundaries, and sharing preferences of other dancers.

  • Do not publish another person's private or sensitive information without authorization.
  • Obtain appropriate permission before recording or sharing content featuring other individuals.
  • Respect the privacy rules of dance studios, instructors, classes, and events.
  • Do not share non-consensual intimate content or use personal information to threaten or harass others.
  • Respect other users' decisions to restrict access, decline invitations, or block accounts.

Be mindful of the personal information, locations, and identifying details you choose to share through community features.

5. Respect Intellectual Property

Dance is built on creativity. Respect the work of choreographers, dancers, musicians, photographers, videographers, and other creators.

Only upload or share content that you own, have permission to use, or are otherwise legally permitted to share. This includes music, choreography, recordings, photographs, and videos.

Give appropriate credit where applicable. Providing credit does not replace any permission or license that may be legally required.

Intellectual property concerns may be submitted through our Support page.

6. Be Authentic

AVOA is intended to support genuine connections and meaningful personal progress.

We do not allow:

  • impersonation intended to deceive or harm others;
  • fraud, scams, phishing, or deceptive schemes;
  • repetitive or unsolicited spam;
  • artificial manipulation of community engagement;
  • malicious links or attempts to compromise other accounts; or
  • misrepresentation of age or attempts to evade account eligibility requirements.

Legitimate promotion of dance classes, events, performances, and creative work is not automatically considered spam, provided it does not involve deceptive, abusive, or disruptive behavior.

7. Participate in Dance Sessions Safely

AVOA may provide features that allow dancers to organize, discover, or participate in dance sessions.

Respect the boundaries, consent, and safety of other participants. Do not use dance sessions or invitations to harass, deceive, exploit, or endanger others.

Use appropriate judgment when meeting people you have connected with online. Consider meeting in established studios or public locations, and avoid sharing unnecessary personal information.

Younger users should involve a trusted parent or guardian when arranging in-person meetings with people they have met online.

8. Reporting and Blocking

If you encounter content or behavior that may violate these Guidelines, you can use AVOA's available in-app reporting features to bring it to our attention.

You can also block other users through available in-app controls to help manage your interactions and community experience.

Reports should be submitted in good faith. Deliberately submitting false or abusive reports to harass other users or manipulate moderation decisions is prohibited.

Additional concerns may be submitted through our Support page or by emailing avoa.dance@gmail.com.

AVOA is not an emergency response service. If you or another person is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency services.

9. Moderation and Enforcement

AVOA uses automated safeguards and manual review to help identify and address prohibited content and behavior. We aim to review reports and respond in a timely manner.

Depending on the nature, severity, and circumstances of a violation, we may take actions including:

  • issuing warnings or requesting changes to content;
  • removing or restricting access to content;
  • resetting profile information that violates our standards;
  • restricting access to certain features;
  • temporarily suspending accounts; or
  • permanently terminating access to AVOA.

Serious violations may result in immediate action without a prior warning, where permitted by law. Repeated violations and attempts to circumvent enforcement actions may result in additional restrictions.

Not every report will result in enforcement action. We consider relevant context, available information, community safety, and applicable legal obligations when reviewing reports.

Report information and moderation records may be retained in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

10. Requesting a Review

We recognize that moderation decisions may sometimes be made in error.

If you believe your content was removed or your account was restricted incorrectly, you may contact us through our Support page to request a review.

Please provide relevant information about the decision and why you believe it should be reconsidered. Applicable notice and appeal rights will be provided where required by law.

11. Changes to These Guidelines

As AVOA and its community evolve, we may update these Guidelines to address new features, safety concerns, and community needs.

We will publish updated Guidelines on this page and revise the "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

For questions about these Guidelines, community safety, content moderation, or enforcement decisions, please visit our Support page or email avoa.dance@gmail.com.

Together, we can make AVOA a place where dancers feel encouraged to create, grow, connect, and celebrate their individual journeys.
