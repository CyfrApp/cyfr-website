THE ROADMAP

This is only the beginning.

CYFR is being built in stages — starting with personal growth and expanding into a worldwide home for dancers.

V1

Track

Journal and log classes, set goals, track progress, save notes, photos, and videos.

V2

Connect

Add friends, tag them in classes, view friends' activity and progress, earn achievements, and build your dancer profile.

V3

Discover

Find classes, studios, competitions, showcases, and events near you, with in-app messaging built into the experience.

V4

Worldwide

Expand CYFR into an international dance platform connecting dancers and communities around the world.
