THE ROADMAP

Where CYFR is going.

Starting with your personal dance journey and growing into a global home for dancers.

VERSION 1

Track Your Journey

Journal and log classes, set goals, track progress, and keep your dance history in one place.

VERSION 2

Connect With Friends

Add friends, tag them in classes, follow their progress, earn achievements, and build your dancer profile.

VERSION 3

Discover Dance Around You

Find classes, studios, competitions, showcases, and events near you, plus connect through in-app messaging.

VERSION 4

Go Worldwide

Expand CYFR into an international platform connecting dancers and dance communities around the world.
